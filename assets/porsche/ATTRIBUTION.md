# Model attribution

Front-fit adaptation: the front carrier alignment, wishbone skins, curved tie
rods, damper frames, spring morphs, caliper lettering, arch/well surfaces and
mounting supports were revised together for game clearance. These are authored
approximations, not manufacturer CAD. The editable, packed Blender source is
included as `Porsche_911_GT3RS_Source.blend`; the runtime code drives its moving
groups. See `docs/PORSCHE_FRONT_FIT.md` for test scope and current asset hashes.

Steering adaptation: front wishbone closure and fixed-length tie rods now use
one shared lateral rack, with geometric steering calibration, different inner
and outer wheel angles, and travel-dependent bump steer. Rolling animation
projects contact velocity onto the posed wheel directions. These are authored
game mechanisms fitted to the retained source, not manufacturer CAD or an OEM
steering-control model.

Rear-detail adaptation: the centered red GT3 RS badge reuses the attributed
Black Snow symbol-atlas contour described below. The fitted 520 x 110 mm rear
plate, carrier, mounting feet, fasteners, stars and generic seals are authored
additions. The fictional registration S GT 992 and country letter use Barlow
Bold outlines under the SIL Open Font License bundled in `fonts/OFL.txt`.
These are visual game details, not a valid registration or official seals.
Porsche reference photographs were used for observation; their pixels are not
included in these additions.

The detailed body and original interior geometry are adapted from **“Porsche GT3
RS” by Black Snow** (https://sketchfab.com/BlackSnow02).

Changes include white/red finish adjustments, authored stock-size red wheel and
PCCB assemblies, component cleanup, a named game-control rig, door/window/pedal/
paddle/steering/wing articulation, hydraulic wing linkages, and conformal side
graphics. The original source UVs and texture details are retained.

Keep the source credit, license link and modification notice with distributions
that include this adapted geometry. The same source information is embedded in
the exported GLB copyright field and CarRoot metadata. No creator endorsement is
implied.

The prior procedural body and the original wheel/rig/graphic additions are
separate authored work. The Three.js and Khronos validator licenses are bundled
with those tools. The one Kolbo-generated comparison mesh has its own provenance
record and is not the source of the detailed body.

Additional adaptation: projected side graphics split between fixed panels and moving doors; new UV projections, embedded rest textures and runtime telemetry artwork on the existing instrument and PCM screen surfaces.

Side-wordmark revision: the slanted GT3 and outlined RS silhouette is derived
from image 7 of the same CC BY 4.0 model's symbol atlas, replacing generic font
text. Source file/image hashes, region and derived contour data are retained in
`references/graphic-source-maps/gt3rs-wordmark.json`. New clipping to the original
paint triangles, normal interpolation, stripe proportions and panel articulation
are project adaptations. The original Black Snow credit and license still apply.

Instrument revision: the central dial annulus and disconnected speed-text quads
are replaced by authored geometry fitted to the source bezel's plane and radius.
New dial artwork uses procedural marks, system-font numerals and the attributed
GT3 RS silhouette. The needle arm, yellow insert, machined hub, shaped LCD,
front bezel lip and thin clear cover are authored additions. Compression/rebound
screen graphics are drawn from reference and remain a visual interpretation;
manufacturer photograph pixels are not used as textures. Source photos and
their credits remain in `references/cockpit-official/manifest.json`.

Suspension adaptation: source arm and hub geometry is mirrored and fitted to the stock wheel/rotor axes; original rear pushrod hardware is replaced with a five-link visual arrangement and conventional spring struts. New bearing spindle/seal hardware, fixed mounts, rear control links, antiroll bars, dampers and spring morphs are authored. Underbody and chassis material roles are separated. Joint datums are inferred from source geometry and reference images, not OEM CAD.

Surface adaptation: new physical paint, twill, textile, rubber, plastic and metal
maps use a separate metre-scaled UV channel. Original artwork UV0 remains.
Leather grain is adapted from [ambientCG Leather026](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Leather026),
released under CC0. It is reduced to 1K, retinted black and given calibrated
roughness for the cockpit. Other new microstructure maps are generated by this
project. Surface scales and colors are visual approximations, not OEM scans.

Cabin revision: local metric UV projections replace stretched detail UVs while
retaining source artwork UV0. Satin interior carbon, finer leather and microfiber,
an approximate uniformly perforated black/red seat fabric, woven belts, buckle
plastic and darker red stitches are adapted or authored by this project.
Official Porsche press photographs in `references/cockpit-official` are reference
only, copyright Porsche AG; their pixels are not used in the asset's textures.
Their source URLs and file hashes are recorded in that directory's manifest.

Chrono revision: the pale dial, source hands/markings and optical pane are replaced
by a fitted authored dark stopwatch dial, satin bezel, two articulated hands,
elapsed-time LCD and thin cover. The source clock hood, base and vents remain.
Artwork is generated by `scripts/create_chrono_textures.py`, without copying
manufacturer photo pixels. Timing ranges and the counter layout are game-oriented
visual approximations, rather than manufacturer CAD, firmware or exact typography.

Exterior carbon revision: a procedural resin micro-normal map and constant packed
roughness/metallic map are authored by this project. The roof, movable upper wing
and front aero flaps receive a bounded physical projection for the twill detail;
their existing source artwork UVs and modeled surface positions are retained.
The coating and weave response are visual approximations, not measured Porsche
material data. No reference photograph pixels are used in these maps.

Forged-wheel revision: the five-way split-spoke webs, red barrels/lips and dark
centre-lock assemblies are authored by this project, using the wheel drawing on
page 3 of [Porsche's published manufacturer certificate](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2023/MC-10246424-0001.pdf)
as a visual reference. Local copies in `references/official-992-wheel-*` remain
reference-only Porsche material. No drawing pixels are used in the asset's
textures. The shape, satin enamel, fillets, tool interface and RS type are
approximations; this is not factory CAD or a mechanically certified wheel model.

Viewer environments are CC0 assets from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license):
[Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09) by Sergej Majboroda and
[Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy)
by Greg Zaal. Local copies are bundled for offline use. Powered by Poly Haven.

Headlamp adaptation: the source rear LED cores, perimeter, outer housing and
fitted covers are retained. The crowded inner module is replaced with an
authored black backplate, four reflector cups, and solid front light guides
positioned from source LED datums. A convex cropped projector surface and
retaining lips are authored. The visible projector core uses an emission image. The
internal refraction is a real-time appearance approximation, not optical CAD
or a photometric simulation. Independent emission roles follow the light
controls while preserving dark reflective surfaces when switched off.
