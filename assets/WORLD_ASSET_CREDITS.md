The safehouse shell and three furnishings are individual CC0 1.0 GLB assets. The included files are unmodified downloads; runtime placement, the operable front door, stairwell, warm light, and collision/locomotion logic are game-side additions.

The source pages identify each asset as public domain under CC0, including commercial reuse and modification. The house is authored at real-world scale. The sofa and steel desk are Poly Haven models distributed through Innerscene; their embedded PBR materials are retained. The bedroom bed has embedded PBR materials as well.

These are modular environment assets, not a scanned complete building interior. The game supplements them with a custom hinged door, measured stair flight and collision volumes, material settings, and localized warm light. The result remains a compact stylized game environment and has not been certified as an architectural visualization.
